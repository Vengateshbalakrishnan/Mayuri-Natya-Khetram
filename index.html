About Mayuri Natya Khetram

❀ ─── ✿ ─── ❀

Nurturing Art and Inspiring Value-Based Lives

Established with the vision of promoting classical Indian art forms, Mayuri Natya Khetram stands as a lighthouse of traditional art in Coimbatore. We guide students from their first steps to advanced university level examinations.

Through our affiliations, our students earn certificates that are recognized by the Government of Tamil Nadu, creating opportunities for academic credit, career growth in art education, and cultural portfolios.

Curriculums

Academic Courses & Milestones

❀ ─── ✿ ─── ❀

Bharatanatyam Curriculum

University Affiliated

Our Bharatanatyam syllabus follows the traditional Melattur style with focus on rhythmic precision (Laya), expressive storytelling (Bhava), and structural purity. Affiliated with Tamil Nadu Dr. J. Jayalalithaa Music and Fine Arts University, students take yearly theoretical and practical examinations to progress through standard grades.

Salangai Poojai

2 to 4 Years

The initial major milestone. Once a student completes the study of all basic Adavus (steps), demonstrating correct posture and rhythm, they receive the Guru's recognition to perform in a dedicated ceremony, wearing the ankle bells (Salangai) for the first time.

Arangetram

8+ Years

The graduation milestone representing solo stage entry. Achieved after at least 8 years of rigorous training, learning complete Margam compositions, and demonstrating absolute mastery. It requires the Guru's formal approval and showcases the dancer’s endurance and artistic depth.

Carnatic Vocal Music

Bridge Academy Affiliated

Our Carnatic Music training is structured in tie-up with Bridge Academy, taking vocal students through a highly systemized 8-Grade syllabus. Training covers vocal exercises, pitch stabilization, rhythmic timing (Tala), and classical compositions from legendary vaggeyakaras.

Grades 1 & 2: Foundations

Focus on Sarali Varisai, Jantai Varisai, Dhattu Varisai, and Alankarams in multiple speeds with pitch maintenance.

Grades 3, 4 & 5: Intermediate

Introduction to Geethams, Swarajathis, and Lakshana Geethas. Understanding basic ragas and talas.

Grades 6, 7 & 8: Advanced Masteries

Varnams, complex Kritis in multiple ragas, and introductory Manodharma Sangeetham (creative improvisation).

Lite Music Curriculum

In-house Grade System

Our Lite Music course is designed for students who want to explore light classical music, devotional songs, bhajan recitals, and popular film melodies. Under the training of Co-Founder Venkatesh Balakrishnan, students learn to control pitch, adapt to karaoke backing tracks, and master mic stage presence.

Vocal Modulation

Level 1 & 2

Voice culture techniques adjusted specifically for light genres, handling soft transitions, range extensions, and vocal dynamics.

Stage Techniques

Performance

Microphone handling, body posture, managing stage fright, and coordination with backing tracks or live accompaniments.

Registration Guidelines

We welcome students of all age groups. Beginners are placed in the Pre-Grade foundational batches, while transfer students with prior dance/music experience will undergo a brief placement session to assess their corresponding grade level (Grades 1 to 8).
